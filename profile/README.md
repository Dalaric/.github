## Dalaric Ltd

Hosting, domains, business email and databases, for small businesses and the
agencies that build for them. One person, Julian Mullins, working from
Dorchester in Dorset.

Most of what I do isn't open source. It's other people's infrastructure, and it
stays theirs. What's here is tooling I built for my own work and had no reason
to keep to myself.

I've run one learning platform since 2000. It's changed hosting more than once
without incident, and been rewritten three times as the practices and the
technology moved on. It now runs across multiple servers on a wide range of AWS
services. That's most of what's worth knowing about how I work.
